# DARIA STUDIO Product Wiki
# DARIA STUDIO 产品 Wiki

This Wiki is the product and delivery baseline for DARIA STUDIO. It translates the approved high-fidelity prototype into explicit requirements, production boundaries, technical decisions, and testable acceptance criteria.
本 Wiki 是 DARIA STUDIO 的产品与交付基线，用于把已经确认的高保真原型转化为明确需求、正式系统边界、技术决策和可测试的验收标准。

The prototype remains the visual and interaction reference. The prototype is not the production architecture and must not be copied directly into the private production repository.
原型继续作为视觉和交互参考，但不是正式系统架构，也不能直接复制到私有正式开发仓库。
