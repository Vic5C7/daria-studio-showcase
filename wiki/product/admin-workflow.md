# Admin Workflow

This document describes the intended staff workflow for managing DARIA STUDIO website
content.

## Primary Staff Flow

1. Staff member opens the admin login page.
2. Staff member signs in.
3. Staff member chooses a content area to edit.
4. Staff member creates or updates draft content.
5. Staff member previews the customer-facing result.
6. Staff member publishes approved content.
7. Customer-facing pages read the newly published content.

## Editable Content Areas

- Service areas
- Service types
- Schools
- Scene types
- Packages
- Clothing add-ons
- Prop add-ons
- Makeup and styling add-ons
- Gallery and media assets
- Customer-facing bilingual copy where approved

## Draft And Publish Rules

- Saving draft content should not immediately affect the public customer site.
- Publishing should be an explicit staff action.
- The system should make it clear whether staff are viewing draft or published content.
- The production design should avoid accidental public changes.
