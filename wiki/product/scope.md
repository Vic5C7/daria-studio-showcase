# Product Scope
# 产品范围

## Product intent
## 产品意图

DARIA STUDIO is a Melbourne photography studio experience that presents selected work, explains photography packages, supports bilingual customer communication, and provides a foundation for customer accounts, bookings, and delivered albums.
DARIA STUDIO 是一个面向墨尔本摄影工作室的客户体验系统，用于展示作品、说明拍摄套餐、支持双语客户沟通，并为客户账户、预约和交付相册提供基础。

## Primary users
## 主要用户

- **Visitor / 访客**: browses the gallery and pricing without an account.
- **Client / 客户**: signs in, reviews package information, communicates requirements, and accesses a personal album.
- **Admin / 管理员**: manages public content, pricing configuration, customers, and client album access.

## MVP scope
## MVP 范围

1. Bilingual public gallery with a stable visual design based on the prototype.
   基于原型稳定视觉设计的双语作品展示。
2. Pricing exploration by service area, service type, school or location, package, and add-ons.
   按服务地区、服务类型、学校或地点、套餐和加购项浏览价格。
3. Customer authentication and role-aware navigation.
   客户认证和基于角色的导航。
4. Staff-facing content and pricing management.
   面向工作人员的内容和价格管理。
5. Customer album viewing, notes, and image download.
   客户相册查看、备注和图片下载。

## Out of scope for this public repository
## 本公开仓库不包含

- Production frontend, backend, CMS, database, authentication implementation, payment, or deployment code.
  正式前端、后端、CMS、数据库、认证实现、支付或部署代码。
- Treating prototype local storage or demo credentials as production behavior.
  将原型的本地存储或演示账号当作正式行为。

## Open product questions
## 待确认产品问题

- Whether the first production release includes booking submission or only package enquiry.
  第一版正式产品是支持提交预约，还是只支持套餐咨询。
- Whether client albums are uploaded by staff, imported from storage, or connected to another delivery system.
  客户相册由工作人员上传、从对象存储导入，还是连接其他交付系统。
- Final service catalogue, pricing, cancellation policy, and payment timing.
  最终服务目录、价格、取消政策和付款时点。
