# Customer Workflow
# 客户工作流

This document describes the intended customer-facing workflow for the DARIA STUDIO website.
本文档描述 DARIA STUDIO 网站面向客户的目标流程。

It focuses on how customers explore the studio, compare services, understand packages, and prepare enquiry information.
它关注客户如何了解工作室、比较服务、理解套餐并准备咨询信息。

## Purpose
## 目的

The customer workflow should help visitors quickly understand whether DARIA STUDIO fits the shoot they need.
客户流程应帮助访客快速判断 DARIA STUDIO 是否适合他们需要的拍摄服务。

The experience should feel visual, clear, bilingual, and lightweight.
体验应保持视觉化、清晰、双语和轻量。

## Primary Customer Flow
## 主要客户流程

1. Customer opens the website.
1. 客户打开网站。
2. Customer recognizes the DARIA STUDIO brand and Melbourne-focused positioning.
2. 客户识别 DARIA STUDIO 品牌和墨尔本相关定位。
3. Customer views selected work samples on the homepage.
3. 客户在首页查看精选作品样片。
4. Customer uses gallery arrows or automatic scrolling to browse images.
4. 客户使用作品箭头或自动滚动浏览图片。
5. Customer opens the pricing page.
5. 客户进入价格页。
6. Customer chooses language if needed.
6. 客户按需切换语言。
7. Customer selects service area.
7. 客户选择服务地区。
8. Customer selects service type.
8. 客户选择服务类型。
9. Customer follows the relevant service flow.
9. 客户进入对应服务流程。
10. Customer reviews estimated price, package details, add-ons, and notes.
10. 客户查看预计价格、套餐详情、加购项和备注。
11. Customer uses the selected information for future enquiry or booking contact.
11. 客户将已选信息用于未来咨询或预约联系。

## Language Flow
## 语言流程

- The default language is Simplified Chinese.
- 默认语言为简体中文。
- Customer can switch between English and Simplified Chinese from the top-right area.
- 客户可以从右上角在英文和简体中文之间切换。
- Language choice should persist after refresh.
- 刷新页面后应保留语言选择。
- Public labels, package details, navigation, and key explanatory copy should be bilingual.
- 公开标签、套餐详情、导航和关键说明文案应支持双语。

## Gallery Flow
## 作品展示流程

- Customer sees the pricing entry button before the gallery.
- 客户在作品展示前看到价格入口按钮。
- Customer sees work samples in a single horizontal row.
- 客户以单行横向方式查看作品样片。
- Images should scroll left automatically at a comfortable speed.
- 图片应以舒适速度自动向左滚动。
- Left and right arrows allow manual browsing.
- 左右箭头允许客户手动浏览。
- Gallery edges should fade images in and out as they enter and leave.
- 图片滚入和滚出时，作品展示边缘应呈现渐入渐出效果。
- Future gallery categories should support service, school, scene, or photo spot browsing.
- 未来作品分类应支持按服务、学校、场景或打卡点浏览。

## Pricing Selection Flow
## 价格选择流程

1. Customer chooses service area.
1. 客户选择服务地区。
2. Customer chooses service type available in that area.
2. 客户选择该地区可用的服务类型。
3. If the selected service is graduation photography, customer chooses school.
3. 如果选择毕业照，客户继续选择学校。
4. If the selected graduation school has scene types, customer chooses scene type.
4. 如果所选毕业照学校有场景类型，客户继续选择场景类型。
5. Customer chooses a package tier.
5. 客户选择套餐档位。
6. Customer chooses optional add-ons where available.
6. 客户选择可用加购项。
7. Customer adds notes where needed.
7. 客户按需填写备注。
8. Customer reviews the persistent estimated total.
8. 客户查看固定显示的预计总价。

## Graduation Photography Flow
## 毕业照流程

- Supported schools currently include The University of Melbourne, Monash University, and RMIT.
- 当前支持学校包括墨尔本大学、莫纳什大学和 RMIT。
- Shared base graduation packages can be reused across supported schools when configured.
- 配置允许时，受支持学校可以共用基础毕业照套餐。
- University of Melbourne can have a dual-scene option with Carlton Garden.
- 墨尔本大学可以有与 Carlton Garden 搭配的双场景选项。
- Package copy should explain original photo counts, retouched photo counts, behind-the-scenes video, and photo spot coverage.
- 套餐文案应说明底片数量、精修数量、花絮视频和打卡点覆盖。
- Photo spot coverage should not overpromise exact final routes under weather, light, crowding, closure, or other sudden conditions.
- 打卡点覆盖不应在天气、光线、人流、封闭或其他突发情况下绝对承诺最终路线。
- Props should reflect selected school availability.
- 道具应根据所选学校显示可用性。

## Registry Wedding Flow
## 注册结婚跟拍流程

- Customer selects Melbourne and registry wedding coverage when the service is available.
- 服务可用时，客户选择墨尔本和注册结婚跟拍。
- Customer compares registry wedding package tiers.
- 客户比较注册结婚跟拍套餐档位。
- Customer can review included coverage, original photo count, retouched photo count, behind-the-scenes video, and location coverage.
- 客户可以查看包含的拍摄内容、底片数量、精修数量、花絮视频和地点覆盖。
- Customer can add extra shoot locations when the selected package supports it.
- 所选套餐支持时，客户可以加拍额外地点。
- Customer can select optional styling, wardrobe, and props where available.
- 客户可以选择可用的妆造、服装和道具加购项。
- Estimated total should update as package, extra locations, and add-ons change.
- 当套餐、额外地点和加购项变化时，预计总价应更新。

## Studio Shoot And ID Photo Flow
## 棚拍与证件照流程

- Graduation photography may include an indoor studio shoot option after requirements are confirmed.
- 需求确认后，毕业照可以包含棚拍选项。
- Studio shoots may offer simple indoor styling as a separate add-on.
- 棚拍可以提供独立的棚内简单妆造加购项。
- ID photos should appear as their own service category once package data and customer copy are confirmed.
- 证件照套餐数据和客户文案确认后，应作为独立服务分类出现。
- If prices or deliverables are not confirmed, the customer-facing page should show a clear pending or coming-soon state.
- 如果价格或交付内容尚未确认，客户页面应显示清楚的待确认或暂未开放状态。

## Notes Flow
## 备注流程

- Customer can add notes for school, scene, package, clothing, props, makeup, or other relevant sections.
- 客户可以为学校、场景、套餐、服装、道具、妆造或其他相关区域添加备注。
- Notes do not affect price unless later explicitly connected to a priced option.
- 备注不影响价格，除非后续明确与收费选项关联。
- Empty notes should not be accepted.
- 空备注不应被接受。
- Each note area can contain up to 10 notes.
- 每个备注区域最多可包含 10 条备注。
- Notes should be suitable for future enquiry or booking submission.
- 备注应适合未来并入咨询或预约提交。

## Customer Output
## 客户输出结果

At the end of the flow, the customer should understand:
流程结束时，客户应理解：

- Which service area and service type they selected.
- 自己选择了哪个服务地区和服务类型。
- Which package tier and add-ons they are considering.
- 自己正在考虑哪个套餐档位和哪些加购项。
- What the estimated total is, if pricing is available.
- 如果价格可用，预计总价是多少。
- Which notes or preferences should be shared with the studio.
- 有哪些备注或偏好需要告诉工作室。
- Whether any selected service is pending confirmation.
- 所选服务是否仍有待确认内容。

## Out Of Scope For The Current Customer Flow
## 当前客户流程暂不包含

- Customer account creation.
- 客户账号创建。
- Online payment.
- 在线支付。
- Confirmed booking submission.
- 正式预约提交。
- Order management or delivery tracking.
- 订单管理或交付追踪。
- Automatic staff assignment.
- 自动分配工作人员。

## Open Questions
## 待确认问题

- Should the first production version include a real enquiry form or only prepare the selected data for manual contact?
- 第一版正式系统是否要包含真实咨询表单，还是只整理已选数据供手动联系？
- Should registry wedding and ID photo services be added to the prototype before production implementation starts?
- 注册结婚跟拍和证件照是否应在正式实现前先加入原型？
- Should gallery category browsing be implemented before the staff media management system exists?
- 是否应在工作人员媒体管理系统之前先实现作品分类浏览？
- How should pending services be displayed: hidden, disabled, or visible with coming-soon copy?
- 待确认服务应如何显示：隐藏、禁用，还是可见并标注暂未开放？
